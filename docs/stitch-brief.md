# Sippy — Stitch brief (single prompt)

הדביקי את הבלוק הבא ב-Google Stitch (Mobile, portrait). מבוסס על Brand Book v3 (Bold Flat / Bright).

---

Design a cohesive set of 3 mobile UI screens for "Sippy", a deliberately minimal, anti-app water tracker: you open it, see today, tap a cup or bottle to add water, done. No account, no history, near-wordless. It supports Hebrew (RTL) and English.

BRAND STYLE (apply to all screens): bold flat graphic — bright and playful but NOT childish; cheeky and confident, with lots of whitespace. Flat color only: no gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D. Thick dark outlines (~3px) on shapes, cards and the glass. Precise geometry; one dominant bright block per screen, the rest calm honeydew and ink.
COLORS: background Honeydew #EAF2E3; ink/near-black #17241F for ALL text and outlines; water & primary Turquoise #35CFC9 (deep #12A49D); action/accent Coral #F25757; highlight Gold #F2CD60; secondary Moss #7DCE82. Text is always ink on a bright color (black-on-color); never small white text on coral.
TYPOGRAPHY: display, logo and big numbers in Rubik (heavy, 700-900); body/UI in Heebo. Numbers are huge and bold with tabular figures.
LOGO: a bold flat water-drop mark (turquoise, lower half deep turquoise as a flat waterline, thick ink outline) next to a lowercase heavy wordmark "sippy".
ICONS: flat line icons, thick ink stroke, rounded joins; cups/bottles as ink outline with a flat turquoise water fill. Actions = icon only; choices = icon + short label.

SCREEN 1 - BRAND / STYLE BOARD: show the logo on light and on dark; the palette as flat swatches with hex + names (Honeydew, Ink, Turquoise, Turq Deep, Coral, Gold, Moss); a type scale (Rubik display sample + a giant "1,450 ml" number + Heebo body sample); core components (coral primary button, outline ghost button, vessel card, the filling glass); and the icon set (cup, mug, bottle, drop, add +, undo, globe, settings, check).

SCREEN 2 - ONBOARDING "Choose your vessels": bold Rubik title "What do you drink from?" + small subtitle. A 2-column grid of selectable vessel cards; each card has a thick ink outline, a flat cup/mug/bottle icon with a little turquoise water fill, a big size number (e.g. 250) and a small unit (ml). Include one dashed-outline "Custom size" card with a + icon. Selected cards get a highlighted outline and a small coral check badge. Bottom: a large bold "Start" button in coral with ink text. Honeydew background, flat, no shadows.

SCREEN 3 - MAIN / TODAY: top bar with the lowercase wordmark "sippy" + drop mark on one side and a small round language globe + settings icon on the other. Center: a small "TODAY" label, a giant bold Rubik number "1,450" with a small "ml" unit, and a smaller tappable line "of 2,000 ml". Hero: a big flat glass (thick ink outline, open top) filled ~65% with flat turquoise water and a flat waterline. Bottom dock: a row of vessel buttons (thick-outline cards showing "250" and "500") plus a coral square "+" add button, and a small subtle undo icon button. Honeydew background, ink text and outlines, flat, bright, generous whitespace, no gradients.

The two most important screens are 2 (Onboarding) and 3 (Today); screen 1 is the design-system reference.
